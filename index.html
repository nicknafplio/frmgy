ScienceCreative Minds

framergy started with a rebellious idea: to reinvent how science can solve the problems of scarce resources. By taking a page out of nature's book, we rationally designed materials that recognize a specific guest molecule with high selectivity; reminiscent of the biomimetic recognition. With designs in hand, we have scaled our materials to ton production capacity. We deliver MOFs with radical stabilities at all pH levels and production costs ready for industrial use.

Synthesis Careful Reactions

Because of the strong electrostatic interaction between the metal nodes and organic linkers in high valence metal MOFs, these frameworks are resistant to water and acids. However, for the same reason, production was limited to small quantities in defective powders. We modeled this substitution reaction and created stepwise processes to promote the flux of ligands. The precise amount of kinetic support for each MOF is protected by framergy under their product classification AYRSORB™.
